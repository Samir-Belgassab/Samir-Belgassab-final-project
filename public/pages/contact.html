Steps for seeking help safely

Four small steps, at your own pace.

STEP 1

Find a safe moment

A time and place where you will not be overheard or interrupted.

STEP 2

Tell someone you trust

A friend, relative, nurse or advocate, in the way you communicate best.

STEP 3

Contact a service

Ask about accessible options: interpreter, text, easy-read or home visit.

STEP 4

Plan next steps

Decide with a support worker what feels safe, and keep key items within reach.

For friends and family

Helpful

  • Listen and believe her
  • Respect her choices and pace
  • Ask how she prefers to communicate

Avoid

  • Confronting the abuser without a safe plan
  • Deciding for her
  • Sharing what she tells you
